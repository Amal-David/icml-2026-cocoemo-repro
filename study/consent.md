# Consent text for the unpublished listening study

Status: draft. Do not present this text to participants until the applicable
ethics, consent, recruitment, compensation, and withdrawal requirements have
been reviewed for the actual deployment jurisdiction.

## What participation involves

You will listen to 30 short generated speech recordings and provide a
naturalness rating and emotion judgments. The study normally takes at least
eight minutes. You may stop before final submission without sending a response.

## Who may take part

You must be at least 18 years old and able to consent for yourself. Do not take
part if listening to emotionally expressive speech would be distressing for you.

## Data collected

The study stores an HMAC of a one-time invitation code, ratings, browser-reported
playback duration, an assigned experimental group, and a server timestamp. It
does not collect your name, email address, account identity, IP address, raw
user agent, microphone data, free text, demographics, or payment details.

Compensation contact records, if used, must be held separately and never linked
to the study-response export.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. The deployment owner must provide a study-specific
withdrawal contact and withdrawal deadline before recruitment. After the stated
deadline, the invitation-code HMAC secret may be destroyed and retained study
records may no longer be practically linkable to an invitation.

## Consent checkboxes

The app requires both confirmations before it reveals any trial:

- I consent to participate in this study.
- I confirm that I am at least 18 years old.

No ethics approval, recruitment authorization, compensation arrangement, or
participant result is asserted by this repository.
